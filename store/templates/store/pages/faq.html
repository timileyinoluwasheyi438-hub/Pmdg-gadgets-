Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying from PMDG.

Do your refurbished laptops come with a warranty?
Yes! All refurbished laptops come with a warranty of up to 12 months. New laptops carry the manufacturer's standard warranty.
How long does delivery take?
In-stock items are dispatched same-day for Lagos deliveries. Nationwide delivery typically takes 2–5 business days depending on your location.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy on all laptops. Items must be in original condition with all accessories included. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
Do you offer installment payments?
Yes, we partner with select buy-now-pay-later providers for eligible customers. Contact us via WhatsApp to learn more about installment options.
Can I inspect the laptop before buying?
Absolutely! You can visit our showroom in Lagos to inspect and test any laptop before making a purchase. Walk-ins are welcome during business hours.
