Our story

Your trusted ally in business technology

Managing information technology for business is not inherently a do-it-yourself prospect. Smart businesses need a trusted technology partner — and that is exactly what PMDG is built to be.

Who we are

PMDG TECHNOLOGY SOLUTION LTD was founded on a straightforward belief: managing information technology for business is like legal advice, accounting, or graphic arts — it is a specialist discipline, not a do-it-yourself task. Smart business people who are not computer hobbyists deserve a quality vendor they can rely on as a trusted ally, not just a box-shifter.

We serve our clients the way a business partner would — with loyalty, transparency, and the economics of an outside vendor. We make sure every client has what they need to run their business with maximum efficiency and reliability. Because many of our clients' information applications are mission-critical, we are committed to being there when they need us most.

PMDG provides both computer products and the services that make them useful. We are especially focused on hardware — Laptops and Desktops — as well as network systems and services for small, medium, and large businesses. Our systems span PC-based LAN environments and minicomputer server-based platforms. Our services include network design and installation, staff training, and ongoing technical support.

Founded as a consulting-oriented value-added reseller (VAR), PMDG evolved into a full reseller to meet the growing market demand for personal computers. Today we differentiate ourselves from price-oriented national chains by placing service and support at the centre of everything we do. We are not just selling hardware — we are building long-term technology partnerships.
